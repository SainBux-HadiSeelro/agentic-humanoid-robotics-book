## Self-Assessment Questions

1. What is the primary benefit of using digital twins in humanoid robotics?
2. How does Gazebo's physics engine contribute to realistic robot simulation?
3. What are the key components of a humanoid robot model in Gazebo?
4. Why are accurate mass and inertia properties important for simulation?
5. What factors affect the stability of humanoid robots in simulation?

## Answer Guide

1. Digital twins allow safe testing of algorithms, reduce physical robot damage risk, and accelerate development cycles.
2. Gazebo's physics engine provides realistic collision detection, contact modeling, and dynamic simulation.
3. Key components include links, joints, visual/collision properties, and inertial parameters.
4. Accurate mass properties ensure realistic physics behavior and stability.
5. Time step, contact parameters, friction coefficients, and joint constraints all affect stability.
